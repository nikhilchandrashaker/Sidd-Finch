Sidd Finch 3D Delivery Simulation

A 3D biomechanics model of the pitching motion for the fictional Mets pitcher from Plimpton’s 1985 Sports Illustrated story. Change how hard each link in the chain fires and watch the throw change.

Delivery

A 3D model of the throwing motion (drag to orbit, scroll to zoom) driven by the five sliders below. Each link fires in order, hip first and wrist last, and hand speed at every instant is the sum of all five contributions. Press play, then move the sliders to reshape the delivery.

The default sliders are calibrated to give 94 mph. This is a prescribed-motion model: it moves the joints on a schedule and adds up their speeds, but it does not compute forces, so it says nothing about arm load or injury risk. Joint speeds are shown as a share of each curve’s own peak, and the orange line is hand speed in mph. In 3D the arm swings in one plane while the pelvis and shoulders turn from side-on to square. That turn is drawn for realism and does not change the numbers.
